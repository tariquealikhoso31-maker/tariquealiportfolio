About me

A bit about who I am

Student, learner, and someone who likes to see an idea turn into a working page.

My name is Tarique Ali Khoso. I'm the son of Israr Ahmed, and I'm from Sindh. I'm currently studying BS Information Technology at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro, where I'm in my 3rd year (6th semester).

I got into web development because it was the first part of IT where I could see the result straight away — write a few lines, refresh the browser, and something changes. That feeling hasn't really gone away. These days I'm working on the front end properly and slowly moving towards the server side too.

My goal is to become a full stack web developer — someone who can take a project from the first sketch all the way to a live website with a working back end and database.

Outside of code, I'm usually with family and friends, or picking up something new on YouTube.
